Gökhan Ersan

  • A graphic designers job is mass communication, not exhibitions.
  • A graphic designer translate information.
  • Advice for Students: You need to show that you can translate information in unique, creative, expressive, clear, and comprehensive ways. Enlarge you range as soon as possible. The worst mistake students can make is only showing work within their comfort zone.
