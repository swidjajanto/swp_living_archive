Thesis Exchange 02

last seen

Prototype 02

A clock says every hour is the same, but it never feels that way. last seen is a Chrome extension and website that records how you move across your computer and draws it back as a living artwork instead of productivity numbers. Cursor paths, pauses, typing rhythm and the sites you pass through build up over the day, then fold into a 24-hour clock. Each hour swells with how much happened in it, and time away appears as dotted lines. Colours follow the sun and the local weather, so you can compare days and ask how the conditions around you shaped your time.
